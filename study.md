## What the top 5 all do (the genre, not a template to copy)

- Masthead: "Saturday 21 November 2026" in huge black serif, dominant. Kicker above
  ("THE WORLD IN ONE DAY") in red caps; a subtitle line below with 3 hero teasers.
- Numbers strip: 6 big red numbers with small labels beneath (13, 3, 6.7, 146,
  $58,983, 124*). Region bar: 7 coloured segments with counts that sum to 35.
- 3 hero cards, each with a DRAWN PICTORIAL illustration on top (globe+thermometer,
  night skyline+missile trails, seismograph+houses), a red label bar, a numbered
  location line, a bold serif headline, 2-line body, named source.
- 10 secondary stories (4-13) in a 5x2 grid, each with a small clear illustration,
  coloured label bar, headline, 1-2 line body, source.
- World map ("WHERE IT HAPPENED") with numbered dots, beside an "ALSO ON SATURDAY
  21 NOVEMBER — 22 MORE STORIES" list (14-35), one complete line each, source named.
- DATE CHECK box (red border) listing what was left out (20 Nov stories).
- Footer: "Every story here happened, or was first reported, on Saturday 21 Nov 2026.
  Sources named beside each story. Original drawings."
- r2/r5 add small verification badges (CONFIRMED = 2+ outlets, OFFICIAL = on the
  record, REPORTED = one outlet) with a legend — a credibility signal ours lacks.
- Typography: serif bold headlines, sans labels/body. Cream paper, red accents,
  dark navy hero art. Restrained palette, generous rules/white space.

## 5 things the top entries do that ours does not

1. CORRECT HERO FACTS/DATES. Our round-2 hero #2 (ICJ: Myanmar committed
   genocide) is a DATE ERROR — verified: the ICJ delivered its judgment on
   Thursday 19 Nov 2026 (Le Monde, CNA, HRW, ICJ press release), not 21 Nov.
   Top entries correctly EXCLUDE it and list it in DATE CHECK. Our hero #1
   ("Kyiv hit again at dawn / Podil") conflates Kyiv with Kryvyi Rih: the real
   21 Nov Ukraine story is the Kryvyi Rih double-tap strike (late 20 Nov, toll
   confirmed 21 Nov): 2 killed (a 7-year-old boy, a 20-year-old paramedic),
   42 injured (Kyiv Independent, Kyiv Post, Ukrinform). Our hero #3 (FBI NCIC
   hack) was first reported Friday 20 Nov (CNN) — top entries exclude it.
   -> Round 3 heroes: COP31 / Riyadh / Peru. Kryvyi Rih as prominent #4.
   ICJ and FBI go in DATE CHECK as excluded, not as stories.
2. LEGIBLE NUMBERS STRIP + REGION BAR. Ours is cramped: the region-bar labels
   overlap/clip ("Europe 8" cut off) and the numbers strip is squeezed. Top 5
   have clean separation, bigger labels, no overlap.
3. PICTORIAL HERO ART. Our hero art is abstract (orange blobs, a blue cylinder)
   and hard to read. Top 5 draw specific scenes (globe+thermometer+clock,
   skyline+missile trails, seismograph+houses).
4. COMPLETE "ALSO" LIST. Ours truncates several entries with "..." (the column
   is too narrow). Top 5 lists are complete one-liners, no truncation.
5. REAL WIRE SOURCES ONLY + VERIFICATION BADGES. Ours uses invented-sounding
   sources ("Jabulani", "OneTurn", "Scoop") that hurt credibility. Top 5 use
   AP/AFP/Reuters/Al Jazeera/USGS/Kyiv Post etc., and r2/r5 add CONFIRMED /
   OFFICIAL / REPORTED badges. Round 3 uses real wires only + badges.
